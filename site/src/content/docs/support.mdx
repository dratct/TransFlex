TransFlex is free, MIT-licensed, and has no paid tier. It is built and maintained in spare time. If it saves you time, any of these helps.

## Chip in

A one-time contribution of any size through PayPal goes straight to the developer.

<LinkButton href="https://paypal.me/truongtc1109" icon="heart" iconPlacement="start">Support TransFlex on PayPal</LinkButton>

Contributing is a thank-you, not a purchase: the app is the same for everyone, and nothing is locked behind it.

## Help without paying

- **Star the project** on [GitHub](https://github.com/dratct/TransFlex). It helps other people find it.
- **Report a bug or ask for a feature** in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/dratct/TransFlex/issues). Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- **Tell someone** who translates a lot on a Mac.
- **Send a fix.** The source is on GitHub and pull requests are welcome.
