Free and open source · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

<Flow title="Type or paste, then read as it streams" shot="popup-streaming" alt="The popup while a translation streams in.">
  Press <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> in any app. Type or paste the text, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⏎</kbd>, and the translation appears word by word beside it. The result is copied for you; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the popup.
</Flow>

<Flow title="Translate the selection without leaving the app" shot="replace-done" alt="A small capsule next to the pointer saying the text was replaced." reverse>
  Give a preset its own shortcuts. **Translate Selection** shows the translation of the selected text in a small popup. **Replace Selection** swaps the selected text for its translation right where you are writing, and leaves the clipboard as it was.
</Flow>

<Flow title="One preset for each kind of text" shot="settings-presets" alt="The Presets tab of TransFlex Settings." darkOnly>
  A preset picks the provider, the model and the instructions. TransFlex starts with Natural EN ↔ VI, Technical Doc EN ↔ VI and Any → English; change them or write your own, each with its own shortcuts.
</Flow>

## Also in the box

- **Images** — drop a screenshot or photo into the popup, paste it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd>, or pick a file, and translate the text in it. Turn on *Reads images* in the preset first.
- **History** — every translation stays on your Mac. Search it, delete entries, or export it as JSON or Markdown.
- **Providers** — OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible server, including one running on your own Mac such as Ollama.
- **Cost on every result** — the popup shows the tokens each translation used and what it cost.

## Your key, your provider

TransFlex has no account and no server of its own. Your API keys live in the macOS Keychain, and your text goes straight from your Mac to the provider you picked. There is no telemetry; the only other connection the app makes is its update check.

## Free and open source

TransFlex is free and MIT-licensed. You pay your AI provider for what you use, and nothing else. If it saves you time, you can [support its development](https://paypal.me/truongtc1109).

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Do I need an API key?</summary>

Yes: a key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini, or the address of an OpenAI-compatible server. The first launch walks you through adding one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work offline?</summary>

Only with a model running on your own Mac behind an OpenAI-compatible server, such as Ollama. The cloud providers need an internet connection.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which languages can it translate?</summary>

Any language the model knows. The presets tell the model what to translate into, so a new language pair is a new preset.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does it ask for the Accessibility permission?</summary>

Only Translate Selection and Replace Selection need it: they copy the text you selected and paste the result back. The popup works without it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there a Windows or Linux version?</summary>

No. TransFlex is a native macOS app; the earlier cross-platform version is discontinued.

</details>
