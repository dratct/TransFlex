<Flow label="01 · Popup" title="Type or paste, then read as it streams" shot="popup-streaming" alt="The popup while a translation streams in.">
  Press <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> in any app. Type or paste the text, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⏎</kbd>, and the translation appears word by word beside it. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> copies it and closes the popup.
</Flow>

<Flow label="02 · Selection" title="Translate the selection without leaving the app" clip="replace" alt="An English sentence is selected in a document and your shortcut is pressed. A small capsule next to the pointer says it is translating, then the sentence turns into Vietnamese and the capsule says it was replaced." reverse>
  Give a preset its own shortcuts. **Translate Selection** shows the translation of the selected text in a small popup. **Replace Selection** swaps the selected text for its translation right where you are writing, and leaves the clipboard as it was.
</Flow>

<Flow label="03 · Presets" title="One preset for each kind of text" shot="settings-presets" alt="The Presets tab of TransFlex Settings.">
  A preset picks the provider, the model and the instructions. TransFlex starts with Natural EN ↔ VI, Technical Doc EN ↔ VI and Any → English; change them or write your own, each with its own shortcuts.
</Flow>

## Also in the box

<Extras>

- **Images**\
  Drop a screenshot or photo into the popup, paste it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd>, or pick a file, and translate the text in it. Turn on *Reads images* in the preset first.
- **History**\
  Every translation stays on your Mac. Search it, delete entries, or export it as JSON or Markdown.
- **Providers**\
  OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible server, including one running on your own Mac such as Ollama.
- **Cost on every result**\
  The popup shows the tokens each translation used and what it cost.

</Extras>

## Private, free and open

<Facts>

- **No account**\
  No server of its own either. Install it, add a key, translate.
- **Your key stays in the Keychain**\
  Your text goes straight from your Mac to the provider you picked.
- **No telemetry**\
  The only other connection the app makes is its update check.
- **Free and MIT-licensed**\
  You pay your AI provider for what you use, and nothing else. If it saves you time, you can [support its development](https://paypal.me/truongtc1109).
- **A native Mac app**\
  Written in Swift, it lives in the menu bar on macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

</Facts>

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Do I need an API key?</summary>

Yes: a key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini, or the address of an OpenAI-compatible server. The first launch walks you through adding one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work offline?</summary>

Only with a model running on your own Mac behind an OpenAI-compatible server, such as Ollama. The cloud providers need an internet connection.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which languages can it translate?</summary>

Any language the model knows. The presets tell the model what to translate into, so a new language pair is a new preset.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does it ask for the Accessibility permission?</summary>

Only Translate Selection and Replace Selection need it: they copy the text you selected and paste the result back. The popup works without it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there a Windows or Linux version?</summary>

No. TransFlex is a native macOS app; the earlier cross-platform version is discontinued.

</details>
