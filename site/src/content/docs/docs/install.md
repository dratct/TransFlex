TransFlex needs macOS 13 or later. One download runs on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Download and open

1. [Download TransFlex for Mac](https://github.com/dratct/TransFlex/releases/latest/download/TransFlex-macos.zip).
2. Double-click the zip to unpack it, then drag **TransFlex** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open TransFlex. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet; choose **Open**.

Every release is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Where it lives

TransFlex is a menu-bar app. It has no Dock icon until you open one of its windows (Welcome, Settings or History), and the icon goes away again when you close them.

Click the menu-bar icon for its menu:

- **Open Popup** shows the translator. The menu shows the shortcut next to it.
- **History** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Y</kbd>)
- **Check for Updates…**
- **Settings…** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>)
- **Quit TransFlex** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>)

Closing a window never quits the app. Use **Quit TransFlex**.

## First launch

A Welcome window walks you through four steps: what the app does, adding an API key, choosing the shortcut that opens the Popup, and where to find the app afterwards. You can skip the key and add it later; see [Quick start](/docs/quick-start/).

## Open at login

Turn on **Settings → General → Startup → Open at login**. It is off by default. If macOS wants your approval, the row says **Needs approval** and offers **Open Login Items…**.

## Updates

TransFlex checks for updates by itself and asks before installing one. When an update is waiting, a dot appears on the menu-bar icon and the menu item reads **Update Available…**.

To check by hand, choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu, or **Settings → About → Check Now**. The same tab has the switch **Automatically check for updates**.
