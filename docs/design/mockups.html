TransFlex — every screen, every state

Sample text throughout

1 · Popup (Open Popup shortcut)

The frame is 60% × 60% of the screen; mockups are scaled to 760 × 380. Source and result share one pane style; the result adds a 2pt accent rule. Each state has exactly one prominent action.

2 · Output-only popup (Translate Selection shortcut)

40% of the screen wide. Height fits the content (160pt … 60% of the screen), top edge fixed. Every new selection starts from its own height, never the last one.

3 · Model picker (Preset editor)

Replaces the long menu. A popover with a focused search field; only text models, grouped by family, newest first; a typed ID can be used as is.

4 · Settings

A grouped Form on every tab: title-case section headers, rows with labels on the left and controls on the right. 640pt wide; the height fits the tab. Numbers use tabular digits, not a monospaced font.

5 · History

Opens in front of Settings and cascades by 24pt. Export and Delete All are enabled only when there are entries.

6 · Welcome, First-run, menu bar

Welcome runs once (560 × 440). First-run is the sheet the popup shows when no provider key exists. The status menu has no item icons.
