TransFlexEvery screen, every state · sample text

Popup

Airy columns: what you wrote on the left, the translation on the right in larger type, directly on a near-opaque material and divided by a fading rule. No boxes. One filled button per state. Shown at 860 × 470 — 60% of a 1440 × 900 screen.

Notices

Errors and requests get their own surface — a notice card in place of the sheet, a strip outside it, or a small banner window — and never sit inside the translation.

Output-only popup

Translate Selection shows just the translation: 40% of the screen wide, as tall as the text (up to 60% of the screen), top edge fixed. Each selection sizes itself from scratch.

Model picker

Opens from the Model row of a preset. Search first; only models that can translate text; grouped by family, newest first.

Settings

A classic Mac preferences window: icon tabs in the toolbar, grouped rows below. Rows carry a label and, where it helps, one line of explanation. The window resizes to each tab.

History

Grouped by day. The detail repeats the popup’s two halves, so a past translation looks like it did when it was made. Opens in front of other TransFlex windows.

Welcome and menu bar

Welcome runs once: four short steps beside a picture of what the app does. The menu bar item is a plain, complete menu.
